About Me

I’m Hussein, a 17-year-old from Baghdad, Iraq, passionate about technology and programming. I started my journey in coding at a young age, around 12, driven by curiosity and a desire to learn and grow. Over time, I have actively developed my skills through hands-on experience. I joined a Full-Stack Development course in 2025, participated in Artificial Intelligence workshops, and completed a Cybersecurity course, all of which helped me explore different areas of technology and expand my knowledge. I have also taken part in a 4-day programming competition organized by Supernova, where I built a complete website from scratch, gaining valuable experience in project planning and execution. Beyond learning, I enjoy sharing what I know with others. I worked as an assistant instructor in both a Programming Basics course and a Full-Stack course at Aoun Company, where I supported students and helped create a positive learning environment. I have built several practical projects and contributed to developing the website of Ishtar Medical Institute, which strengthened my problem-solving skills and gave me real-world development experience. I’m always eager to learn, take on new challenges, and continue growing both personally and professionally.
